<objective>
Prove the whole dashboard drawer path end-to-end on one thin slice: Super+D →
`shortcuts.json` manifest → Lua keybind → shell-root `LazyLoader` → a
`quickshell-dashboard` `PanelWindow` with locked geometry → family + per-surface
layer rules → focus grab → every dismissal path → destroy-on-dismiss → silent
fullscreen refusal, with a single placeholder pane standing in for content.

This is the phase's **tracer** (`TRACER_MODE=true`). It is production quality, not a
prototype: the surface it creates is the surface plans 14-03..14-09 build inside, the
namespace it picks is the one Phases 15 and 16 inherit, and the constants it declares
on the drawer root are the ones every later widget reads. Everything it stubs is a
content gap that fills without an architectural change — the placeholder pane is
replaced by 14-03's `TabBar` + `SwipeView`, nothing else consumes it.

It runs before every expansion plan so an architectural dead end (layer posture,
focus mechanics, geometry, namespace matching) surfaces after one commit rather than
after ten.

Purpose: DASH-01 and DASH-08, and the "overlay by default, zero exclusive zone"
layer convention the ROADMAP names as this phase's Owns clause.
Output: `modules/Dashboard.qml`, its `qmldir` registration, the third
`shortcuts.json` entry, the Super+D bind, the `quickshell-*` layer rules, and the
shell-root loader/shortcut/guard.
</objective>
