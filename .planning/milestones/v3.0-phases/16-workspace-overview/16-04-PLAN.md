<objective>
Close the two failure modes this phase's ROADMAP entry says it *owns*, on the gate
side rather than the eyeball side.

The first is silently-denied screencopy rendering blank tiles instead of erroring.
Hyprland does not error when a client is not permitted to capture — it hands back
nothing, and every thumbnail goes blank. `quickshell-doctor` gains a check that
asks the surface itself how many of its thumbnails have content, so the blank-grid
failure is machine-detectable rather than dependent on someone noticing.

The second is `ecosystem:enforce_permissions` silently reverting. The setting is
read once at compositor startup and its failure mode is silence, so a check that
reads it back at runtime is the only thing standing between "we enabled
enforcement" and "we believe we enabled enforcement". This plan also *performs*
the live-enforcement proof Phase 11 deliberately deferred here — enable it, start
a genuinely fresh session, and confirm all four screencopy consumer paths still
work, including `gpu-screen-recorder`, which Phase 11 flagged as unconfirmed and
refused to guess at.

Everything here follows this repo's house rule: a gate must be proven able to fail
before it is trusted to pass. Seven checks, seven poisoned fixtures, plus one live
falsification the fixtures cannot express.

Purpose: OVER-01's live thumbnails stay honest after this phase closes, and the
permission system this repo has shipped inert since Phase 11 becomes real.
Output: an extended `quickshell-doctor`, committed fixtures, and enforcement
enabled behind a real session-restart proof.
</objective>
