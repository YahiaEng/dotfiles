<objective>
Take the measurement OVER-04 exists to take, and record the verdict honestly.

The requirement's own wording is the shape of the work: performance "either stays
inside [the budget] or ships the documented fallback instead — the measurement and
the verdict are both recorded". So this is not "make live capture fast". It is
"try live, measure honestly, and if it misses, descend a ladder that was
pre-authorised at decision time so no new decision is needed — and write down which
rung you landed on and why".

The load being measured is real and named: each capture view is an independent
stream, the compositor copies a buffer per frame per client, and on this host that
can mean fifteen concurrent streams from a 165Hz monitor with windows up to
2560x1440 — under a full-screen blur roughly five times larger than any region this
shell has blurred before. The budget is a 60fps floor, a 165fps target, and the
shell under half of one CPU core, measured with at least eight windows across at
least three workspaces. Landing between the floor and the target is a result, not a
failure; that distinction was decided deliberately so the ladder fires on a visible
problem rather than on a number.

This plan also opens the running use note D-16-22 discharges criterion 5 with. The
overview is the highest-frequency new surface in this milestone, which is exactly
why it may not close on a single click-and-look — and why the note's closure
condition is "entries spanning three calendar days", a thing a script can check
rather than a thing someone can feel good about. D-16-22 also states the
scheduling consequence plainly, so it is repeated here rather than discovered
later: this phase cannot close on the day it is built, and Phase 17 sits behind it.

Purpose: OVER-04 becomes a recorded measurement with a recorded verdict, and
criterion 5's multi-day discipline is started rather than skipped.
Output: a measurement document, a ladder disposition, and a dated note with a
deliberate first entry.

<assumption_delta_decision>
**Signal:** `pluralization` on *another* ("Dragging a window thumbnail onto
**another** workspace tile moves that window there") and on *fallback*
("…either stays inside it or ships the documented **fallback** instead").

**The noun that is now primary.** The primary key for every operation in this
phase is **(window identity, workspace-target token)** — `HyprlandToplevel.address`
paired with a workspace token that is either an integer `1..10` or the literal
`special:magic`. "The focused window on the current workspace" is demoted to one
*instance* of that pair. Decision: `promote`, unchanged across the phase and now
guarded by the single-dispatch-call-site criterion in plan 16-07 and the
single-capture-path check in plan 16-04.

**Second signal — render mode, and this plan is where it is cashed in.** The
`fallback` term in OVER-04's own wording is the second pluralization: live capture
and snapshot capture are two ways of drawing the same thumbnail. Decision:
`promote` — and this plan is the proof that the promotion was the right call. If
the verdict is `BELOW-BUDGET`, rungs 2 and 3 are changes to the existing
capture-mode variant on the one general representation, not a second renderer
written under time pressure at the end of a phase. Rung 1 does not touch the
representation at all. The cost of having promoted early is one extra property in
plan 16-03; the cost of not having promoted would have been discovering, at this
task, that "live" was baked into the thumbnail's structure and the fallback needed
a parallel path.

**Deliberate non-generalisation, recorded.** The mode is baked in at build time
rather than exposed as a runtime axis. That is *not* an add-alongside: it is one
representation with one variant selected at build time, shipping one code path.
Making it runtime-switchable would mean three tested behaviours, config that
probably never gets used, and a self-adapting shell that oscillates under load —
which would also make this very measurement irreproducible. The generalisation
lives in the representation; the selection stays singular on purpose, and the
ladder is written down so changing it later is a small edit rather than a
rediscovery.

**Accepted debt: none.** Both signals end the phase promoted and both promotions
are mechanically guarded.
</assumption_delta_decision>
