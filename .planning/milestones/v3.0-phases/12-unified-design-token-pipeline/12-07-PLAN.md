<objective>
Retrofit wleave — the GTK4 proof surface — to consume the emitted motion tokens, remove its
lint exemption, and judge the result with a blocking human render-and-look gate before the
plan closes.

Purpose: D-19 chose wleave for a specific reason. It is GTK4, so the emitted `cubic-bezier`
is proven on the toolkit that renders it natively, and it already hand-copies Hyprland's
`md3_decel` four numbers *with a comment saying so* — so retrofitting it demonstrates the exact
drift this pipeline eliminates rather than a synthetic case. D-27 puts the human gate here
rather than at phase end because wleave is a regression risk on a surface already approved once,
and that is best judged against fresh memory rather than after unrelated work.

Output: a token-consuming `style.css`, one fewer lint exemption, and a recorded human verdict on
whether the feel regressed.

Scope fence: wleave is the ONLY GTK surface retrofitted this phase. waybar, swaync, walker,
SwayOSD and the AGS media card stay untouched — Phase 13 owns that sweep, and three of them have
no CSS custom-property mechanism at all on the installed GTK3.
</objective>
