### Manifest schema (tab-separated, `#` comments allowed, one row per pair)

`ID · PLAN · UNIT · ARTIFACT · CONSUMER · SCOPE · REF · MODE · NOTE`

- **`ARTIFACT`** — a declarative predicate from a fixed five-word vocabulary, never a shell expression: `exec:<path>` (exists and is executable), `file:<path>`, `dir:<path>`, `sym:<path>::<literal>` (file exists and contains the fixed-string literal), `none`.
- **`CONSUMER`** — a repo-relative path, or `-` for rows with no consumer.
- **`SCOPE`** — `file` (whole file), `array:<NAME>` (only inside the `<NAME>=(` … `)` block, the shape 17-04's own criteria use), or `nocomment` (comment-stripped first).
- **`REF`** — a fixed string, or `re:<ERE>` for the array-scoped entries.
- **`MODE`** — `pair` | `branch` | `warn` | `empty` (defined below).
- **`NOTE`** — free text, quoted into the report for any non-`[OK]` row.

### The four verdict states (`MODE=pair`)

"Finished" is defined mechanically as **the producing plan's SUMMARY exists** — `.planning/phases/17-ambient-extras/17-0N-SUMMARY.md`. That is GSD's own completion contract, it is the same marker the orchestrator uses, and it is exactly the line criterion 3 draws: a plan without a SUMMARY is work the phase *started and did not finish*.

| `plan_finished` | artifact | ref | Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| yes | present | present | `[OK]` | finished and wired |
| yes | absent **or** ref absent | — | `[FAIL] incomplete` | the plan reports done but half its wiring is missing |
| no | absent | absent | `[OK] not started` | nothing was begun; nothing to sweep |
| no | present **or** ref present | — | `[DRIFT]` | **the criterion-3 violation** — a consumer carries a reference to something started and not finished. Report file, line, and the remediation |

`MODE=branch` ignores `plan_finished` and asserts only artifact↔ref **symmetry** (both or neither), for pairs whose existence is a recorded branch rather than a completion state. `MODE=warn` never produces a verdict — it reports state and a suggested operator command. `MODE=empty` asserts zero matches.
