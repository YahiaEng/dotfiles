## `<assumption_delta_decision>` — primary noun `service participant`, decision `promote`

**This plan carries the phase's primary assumption-delta decision.** The detector fired
`kind: pluralization` on the ROADMAP's *Owns* clause for Phase 15: *"three new PipeWire /
NetworkManager / BlueZ consumers arriving at once **alongside** existing owners."*

**Primary noun (generalized identity): `service participant`.**

The current identity model in `quickshell-doctor` knows exactly one noun — **owner** —
and asserts cardinality 1 on it. Two shipped checks encode that assumption: the single
`org.freedesktop.Notifications` owner check (lines 295-304) and the XF86 key-ownership
check (lines 307-330). This phase introduces three participants that are emphatically
*not* owners: they are unbounded **clients** of PipeWire, NetworkManager and BlueZ.
D-15-24's own reasoning already draws that line — *"the panels are additional consumers,
not ownership claimants"* — but no instrument encodes it.

**Decision: `promote`.**

Generalize the doctor's D-Bus model so `service participant` is the primary noun, with
two variants demoted to details:

| Variant | Cardinality | Asserted? | Poison-provable? | Members after this plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `name-owner` | exactly 1 | yes | yes | `org.freedesktop.Notifications` (expected owner `swaync`) |
| `client-consumer` | N | **never** asserted exclusive | n/a — the assertion is that no exclusivity assertion exists | PipeWire, NetworkManager, BlueZ |

Register the three new panels as `client-consumer`s of PipeWire / NetworkManager / BlueZ
so a future check cannot accidentally assert exclusivity over a many-client service.

**Rationale (carried, not summarized):** without the promotion, D-15-25's new checks would
be written against the only noun that exists — `owner` — and would either accidentally
assert exclusivity over three many-client services, or would be bolted on as unnamed
one-off greps that the next phase cannot reuse. The promotion costs one data structure and
one structural guard; the alternative costs a false failure the first time two clients
legitimately talk to PipeWire at once, which is PipeWire's designed operating mode.

**Reversibility: costly.** The two variant names enter the repo's diagnostic vocabulary,
the four check names become a published contract that 15-08's waybar work and Phase 16's
overview both read, and `QSD_CLIENT_CONSUMERS` is the registry a later phase extends
rather than re-invents. Flagged, not gated — the decision is the outline's, already made,
and gating a decision already made is the anti-pattern.

**The secondary `add-alongside` observation belongs to 15-02 and is already recorded
there** (the `summonable layer surface` noun, `PanelDialog` as its promoted representation
for new surfaces only, `Dashboard.qml` deliberately not refolded, accepted debt). It is
deliberately **not** duplicated here as a decision.
</assumption_delta_decision>
