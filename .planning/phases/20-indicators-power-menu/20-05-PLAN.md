<objective>
Expand the proven OSD path from one control to the full QOSD-04 column, and add the QOSD-02 Caps
Lock variant that has no reference-shell precedent to copy.

Purpose: the tracer in plan 20-04 proved the frame, the namespace, the blur, the state-driven
trigger and the dwell. This plan adds the two things that actually make the OSD better than the
pill it replaces: independently adjustable sliders that only appear for controls that genuinely
moved, and a Caps Lock indicator with no root service anywhere in its path. Neither Caelestia nor
end-4 has a caps-lock indicator, so QOSD-02 is this project's own to preserve.
