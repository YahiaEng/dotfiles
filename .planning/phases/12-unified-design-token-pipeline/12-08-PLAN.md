be meaningless: `spring`/`damping`/`mass` is a physical spring-constant parameterisation, and

starting values are therefore tuned by feel, and D-27's human render-and-look gate is the actual
arbiter, not a match against a borrowed constant. RESEARCH's Assumptions Log A1 records that MD3
