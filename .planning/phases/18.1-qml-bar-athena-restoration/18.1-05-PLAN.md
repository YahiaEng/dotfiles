<objective>
Change both bar drawers from click-toggle to hover-reveal, with a grace period across the trigger-to-drawer gap, explicitly integrated with `BarReveal`'s existing hot-zone state machine rather than merely coexisting with it.

Purpose: "nothing expands like Athena that shows my utilities" is one of the operator's four named complaints, and it is the cheapest of the four to close — the drawers already exist and are complete. Only the trigger is wrong. The risk is not the feature; it is putting a second hover system on a surface that already has one.
Output: apps and settings drawers that open on hover and stay open across the pointer gap, and that cannot open into a bar that is mid-reveal or mid-hide.
</objective>

Add a local hover-reveal mechanism to `LauncherCapsule.qml`. It is deliberately local per RESEARCH's "Don't Hand-Roll" row: reuse `PopoutController`'s TOKENS, never its allowlist or its state machine.

Declare `property bool drawerHoverActive: false` and a `function reportDrawerHover(source, entered)` that records the hovered state of each of the two hover sources — the trigger cell and the drawer strip — and sets `drawerHoverActive` to their disjunction. Both surfaces must feed the same state through this one function; if the trigger and the strip each drove their own boolean, the pointer travelling from trigger to strip would register a clean exit and collapse the drawer mid-motion, which is exactly what D-17's grace exists to prevent and what a per-surface boolean would defeat before the grace ever ran.

Declare `readonly property bool drawerSettled` resolving to the bar being rendered and not mid-transition, read from `Bar.qml`'s existing `barRendered` and `barTransitionRunning` per D-26. Do not read, write, extend or mirror `PopoutController.barSettled` — it is a dead latch and copying its shape would produce a second one.

Add two `Timer`s, both `repeat: false`:
- `drawerDwellTimer`, `interval: Design.popoutDwellMs`, armed when `drawerHoverActive` becomes true. On trigger it calls `requestExpand()` ONLY IF `drawerHoverActive` is still true AND `drawerSettled` is still true. Both conditions are re-evaluated at fire time, not merely checked when the timer was armed — a dwell armed while the bar was up must not open a drawer into a bar that began hiding 300ms later.
- `drawerGraceTimer`, `interval: Design.popoutDismissGraceMs`, armed when `drawerHoverActive` becomes false. On trigger it calls `requestCollapse()` ONLY IF `drawerHoverActive` is still false. Re-entering the trigger or the strip within the grace window stops this timer and leaves the drawer open.

Attach `HoverHandler`s reporting into `reportDrawerHover` on the trigger cell and on `stripHost`, and REPLACE the trigger's `MouseArea` click toggle at line 202-205 rather than leaving it alongside the hover path. Athena's `group/apps` has `"transition-duration": 500` and no click action at all; D-16 says only the trigger changes, and a click-pin living beside a hover-reveal means a pointer exit can collapse a drawer the user believed pinned.

Add an explicit collapse on the bar going away: when `drawerSettled` goes false while the drawer is expanded, stop both timers and call `requestCollapse()` immediately, with no grace. A drawer that survived into a hidden bar would reappear expanded on the next reveal, which is QBAR-07's boundary case.

Do not write to `BarReveal` at all — not `reportHover`, not `revealOverride`, not any of its state. `Bar.qml`'s existing `barHoverHandler` already spans the whole bar content area and already reports the bar as hovered, and the drawer expands INSIDE that same window, so the bar's re-hide protection already covers an open drawer with zero new wiring. Adding a second reporter would double-count.

Change nothing about the drawer's contents: `appEntries`, `LauncherCell`, its `IconImage`, `Quickshell.iconPath()`, `resolvedEntry`, `resolvedIconPath`, the `apps` fallback glyph, `expandedCrossExtent`, `cellPitch`, `stripHost`'s width/height bindings and both `Behavior` blocks are all untouched.
