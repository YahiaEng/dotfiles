## Root Causes Found (recorded, not fixed)

A subsequent pixel-level investigation (real `grim` screenshot, histogram comparison against Athena) root-caused all four complaints:

1. **Black bar surfaces — a type bug.** `quickshell/.config/quickshell/modules/Colours.qml:106` declares alpha-blended roles (feeding `capsule`/`capsuleHover`/`barSurface`/`barSurfaceHover`/`capsuleTrack`) as `property string`, not `property color`. `BarRoles.qml`'s blend expression reads `.r`/`.g`/`.b` off that string — undefined on a JS string — so `Qt.rgba(undefined, undefined, undefined, 0.85)` evaluates to opaque black. The 12 non-alpha roles are unaffected because QML auto-converts a hex-string literal when assigned directly to a `color` property, which is exactly why the filled pills render correctly while every translucent surface does not. Pixel evidence: Athena's bar strip contains `#323A4F` (the expected 0.85·`#313244` over wallpaper) at 12,299px; the QML bar's dominant fill is `#010E1C` at 50,908px — black composited over the wallpaper.
2. **Oversized/clunky geometry.** `Design.iconSizeMd` (24px) vs Athena's glyph-only 16px; capsule inner padding 8px (`Design.spacingSm`) vs Athena's `6px 6px`. Both were found during this phase's own Athena audit and filed as out-of-scope — that filing was wrong, since together they are the "unmistakably larger" complaint. The pacman/updates glyph is also not optically centred.
3. **Sudden drawer expansion.** Motion is present and enabled (`motion.json` `motion_enabled: true`, 375ms emphasized-in MD3 bezier; `LauncherCapsule.qml:308-323` has `Behavior on width`/`Behavior on height`). The container animates, but the revealed cells appear instantly with no fade/stagger, reading as sudden next to Athena's `transition: all` on the members themselves. Not definitively isolated as the sole cause — recorded as a lead, not a proven single defect.
4. **Jarring default app-drawer icons.** `LauncherCapsule.qml:50-57`'s `appEntries` IS the correct predetermined 7-app list matching Athena's `custom/app-{zen,spotify,discord,steam,lutris,obsidian,codium}` set. The divergence is in icon resolution: this phase's D-18-01 icon-theme redesign resolves each entry through the desktop-entry/icon-theme database, so entries without a good themed icon fall back to a generic glyph, while Athena hardcodes one font glyph per app. D-18-01's redesign is the direct cause and needs revisiting.

## Verification-Method Gap (recorded explicitly)

No alpha-blended `BarRoles` role was ever probed for a **resolved** value across this phase's automated checks. Plan 18.1-01's own verification asserted `typeof BarRoles.accent === "object"` — a non-alpha role — and never exercised a blended one. Plan 18.1-06's D-20 `quickshell-doctor` colour-routing check only asserts that no bar file holds a direct `Colours.*` reference; it does not assert that a routed role RESOLVES to a real colour. That is why a 100%-black bar passed every automated check in this phase, including the newest check built specifically to guard bar colour correctness. The check is not wrong — it is insufficient. A future fix needs a resolved-value assertion on every alpha role, not just a routing/reference check.
