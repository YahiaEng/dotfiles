  <what-built>
Phase 18.1 restored Athena's visual language to the Quickshell QML bar, against the operator's own verdict that it looked "nothing like my Athena waybar… so much worse. No colors, big and clunky, nothing expands like Athena that shows my utilities, the workspace only has icon for terminal."

- **Colour architecture.** A new `modules/bar/BarRoles.qml` singleton mirrors Athena's `theme.scss` with all seventeen roles and all five alpha values byte-exact. Every bar capsule routes colour through it; a new `quickshell-doctor` check enforces that and has been proven able to fail.
- **Filled pills.** Athena's three filled hues are back: accent on the focused workspace, secondary on the clock, primary on the notification bell when there is something unread. Capsules are translucent again rather than opaque grey.
- **Workspace glyphs.** Athena's three-state glyph set replaces the numeral, and all twelve app glyphs are filled in — seven of them were literally empty strings in source, which is why only the terminal ever showed an icon.
- **Hover-reveal drawers.** The apps and settings drawers open on hover with a grace period across the trigger-to-drawer gap, explicitly integrated with the bar's existing hot-zone reveal.
- **Resource pills.** cpu, ram and disk are icon-only at rest and reveal their value in place on click, with Athena's own warning and critical thresholds.
- **Tray removed**, matching Athena, which dropped it because the applet icons duplicated the connections group.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
**Setup — both bars, at the same time.** Bring up the Athena waybar alongside the running QML bar so the two can be seen together. If Athena cannot be brought up at all, STOP and report it: a one-sided comparison is not the judgment D-23 asks for, and this gate does not pass by default.

**Then walk the operator's own four complaints, in their own words:**

1. *"No colors."* Look at the bar as a whole. Is colour arriving as fill — a solid accent pill on the focused workspace, a coloured clock, a coloured notification pill when there is something to notify — rather than as tint on grey?
2. *"Big and clunky."* The geometry is deliberately unchanged and measured identical to Athena's, so what should have changed is density and weight: translucent capsules instead of opaque slabs, no always-on `4% 36% 7%` text, one fewer capsule. Does it feel lighter?
3. *"Nothing expands like Athena that shows my utilities."* Rest the pointer on the apps glyph, then on the gear. Do both drawers open on hover? Move the pointer across the gap into a drawer — does it stay open? Move away — does it close after a beat? Click a settings axis — does its picker open and the drawer close?
4. *"The workspace only has icon for terminal."* Open a browser, a terminal and a file manager on different workspaces. Does every one show a real glyph rather than nothing or a box? Does the focused workspace read as a filled pill?

**Then check nothing that worked before has broken:** scroll on audio and brightness; click a workspace to switch to it; click a bar section to open its popout; click a resource glyph to reveal its value and again to hide it; confirm the bar auto-hides and reveals on hover and on holding Super; confirm the reserved screen edge has not drifted.

**Four named deltas — stated up front so the judgment is informed, not surprised:**
- The workspace **numeral is gone**, replaced by a state glyph. This loses the numeral's one-to-one visual mapping onto the Super+N keybinds. Accepted deliberately in D-08; it is real and it is a cost.
- Athena has a **fourth workspace state** (`empty`, its own glyph at reduced opacity) that D-08 did not name and this phase did not implement. A workspace with no windows shows the `default` glyph.
- **Five persistent workspace slots, not six** — carried forward from Phase 18, three of Athena's four layouts use five.
- **The tray is gone**, and QBAR-05 is recorded Withdrawn. Athena dropped it for the same reason.

**The question:** running these side by side, is the QML bar **at least as good** as the Athena waybar?

A verdict of **exactly equal passes** — "at least as good" makes the tie a pass. Only "worse" fails. Judge the tie deliberately rather than letting it default.

A **FAIL is a legitimate and useful outcome.** It keeps 18-19 and 18-20 blocked and the waybar installed, which is exactly the position this gate exists to protect. If it fails, say what specifically is still worse — that is the input the next phase needs.
