## Accomplishments

- Added `clockFillPill`, a `Rectangle` filled with `BarRoles.fillClock` (Athena's `secondary` hue) behind the clock's time and weekday `Text` elements, both rebound from `contentColour` to `BarRoles.fillClockFg`. ## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Avoided reproducing the Grid-positioner-forbids-anchors defect before it shipped**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (clock fill pill), while re-reading the plan's literal placement instruction against the prior-wave context's own warning about this exact failure class.
- **Issue:** The plan's `<action>` text describes `clockFillPill` as a `Rectangle` "behind the clock's content", which reads naturally as a child declared inside `clockTriggerGrid` (a `Grid` positioner). Plan 18.1-02 already hit this exact defect live (`Cannot specify anchors for items inside Grid. Grid will not function.`) when a fill Rectangle used `anchors.fill` as a direct Grid child.
- **Fix:** Declared `clockFillPill` as a sibling of `clockTriggerGrid` (both direct children of `PopoutTrigger`'s plain, non-positioner `contentHost` Item), anchored via `anchors.centerIn: clockTriggerGrid` with explicit `width`/`height` bound to `clockTriggerGrid.width`/`height` plus padding — Grid positioners auto-size to their content, so this reads the Grid's real (not implicit) dimensions safely.
- **Files modified:** `quickshell/.config/quickshell/modules/bar/ClockActionsCapsule.qml`
- **Verification:** `systemctl --user restart quickshell.service` after the edit produced a clean `Configuration Loaded` with zero new `WARN`/`ERROR` lines in `$HOME/.cache/quickshell.log`.
- **Committed in:** `cefe1a6` (Task 1 commit)
