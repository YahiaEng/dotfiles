## Decisions Made

- WINDOWS.md closure status is `fixed`, not the plan's literal `resolved` — `resolved` is not a member of gsd-core's validated ledger status enum (`open`/`waived`/`fixed`); writing it would have broken the next `gsd-tools windows` parse. `fixed` is the schema-valid substitute; the honest "closed by deletion, not by a performed verification" nuance lives in the `reason` column, which the schema does allow free text in regardless of status.
- QBAR-05's checklist marker uses `- [W]` (no prior third-state convention exists in this file — only `[x]`/`[ ]` appear anywhere) rather than reusing `[ ]`, which would misleadingly read as "not yet done" instead of "deliberately withdrawn."
- Live restart verification (quickshell.log load-error check, visual bar-renders-complete check) deliberately deferred per this project's established skip-live-verification preference, and disclosed as a new WINDOWS.md entry rather than silently omitted.
