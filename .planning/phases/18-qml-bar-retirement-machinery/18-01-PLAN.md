<objective>
Ship the phase's tracer: one production-quality `Bar.qml` `PanelWindow` that mounts
unconditionally at `shell.qml`'s root, claims a real exclusive zone, and renders exactly
one real capsule — the live clock — wired end-to-end and proven on the live host.

Purpose: this phase's architecture rests on three properties no surface in this repo has
ever had. Proving all three on one thin slice, before nineteen further plans commit to
them, is what turns an architectural dead-end into a one-commit correction instead of a
ten-layer rollback. The three properties, named here because every later plan inherits
them:

1. **First surface with `exclusiveZone > 0`.** Every prior Quickshell surface
   (`Dashboard`, the three panels, `Overview`) sets `exclusiveZone: 0`. The reserved-space
   arithmetic (D-18-38) has never been exercised in this repo and the value it must match
   is a live baseline, not a formula anyone has run.
2. **First surface with no dismissed state.** Every prior surface is LazyLoader-summoned
   and destroyed on dismiss, so it inherits the zero-idle discipline for free. The bar
   inherits none of it — anything it schedules runs for the whole session. This plan's
   clock is deliberately event-driven for that reason, and Task 3 captures the cost floor
   while the bar is still one capsule, because after wave 3 there is no minimal bar left
   to measure.
3. **First surface that must survive its own process death.** Out of scope here; QBAR-10's
   restart unit (18-07) is the mitigation. Named so the executor does not mistake the
   absence of a restart path in this plan for an oversight.

Output: `modules/Bar.qml`, four appended `Design.qml` tokens, a `modules/qmldir`
declaration, an unconditional `shell.qml` mount, and `18-BAR-IDLE-BASELINE.md`.

Explicitly NOT in this plan (each owned by a named later plan, listed so the executor does
not helpfully build ahead): the entry-list model and `BarCapsule.qml` extraction (18-05),
the `bar/` component directory (18-05), any other capsule (18-08/09/10/11), vertical
orientation (18-05), popouts (18-13/14), auto-hide and the `bar` IPC handler (18-15),
the hot zone (18-16), doctor checks (18-17), the restart unit (18-07).
