<objective>
Mint GATE-04: `colour-lint`, a new deny-by-default QML colour gate anchored narrowly on
colour-assignment context, mirroring `motion-lint`'s CHECK A/B/C + EXEMPTIONS shape, folded
blocking into `theme-doctor`'s tally.

Purpose: `waybar-design-lint`'s CHECK D ("no literal hex") is deleted by RETIRE-02 later in

before 18-05 through 18-16 create roughly a dozen new QML files — so that no bar surface is
ever born outside the lint. Minting it after the capsules land would mean auditing them
retroactively; minting it now means every one of them is judged by construction.

file" scan false-positives on `Colours.qml`'s own 19 `"#FF00FF"` `JsonAdapter` fallback
defaults and is therefore unusable on day one. The whole design turns on scoping the match to
colour-ASSIGNMENT context so those 19 fall outside it without needing an exemption at all —
which is a stronger invariant than exempting the definition source from its own lint.

Output: `hypr/.config/hypr/scripts/colour-lint`, six committed fixtures under
`tests/colour-fixtures/`, and a blocking fold block in `theme-doctor`.

ahead: `retirement-check` and its own fold (18-06, RETIRE-01/D-18-35); `quickshell-doctor`'s
GATE-03 structural checks including the `_qsd_check_reserved_array_manifest_coverage`

this plan writes no `.qml` outside the fixtures directory, and in particular does not create
