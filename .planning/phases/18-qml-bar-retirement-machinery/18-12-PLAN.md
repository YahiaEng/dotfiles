## Where the brightness section lives, and why it is an entry rather than a capsule

D-18-39 calls it a "brightness section" and says it is "the exact D-18-06 battery precedent." Battery
is an **entry** inside the `mediaConnectivity` capsule, not a capsule of its own. A seventh capsule
would contradict D-18-10's 5–6 by-concern shape and UI-SPEC's locked six-capsule split, and would give
brightness a heavier presence on the bar than the battery it is modelled on. So brightness is an entry
in `BarEntryModel`'s `mediaConnectivity` capsule, placed **immediately after audio** so the two
scroll-adjustable entries are contiguous — one scroll zone rather than two separated by three
non-scrollable glyphs — leaving `battery` last where the model already has it.

**Explicitly NOT in this plan** (each owned by a named plan, listed so the executor does not build
ahead): every popout body, including the audio popout with its volume slider (18-13/18-14); the hover
dwell, the pin latch and the one-open-at-a-time summon (18-13); any change to the five entries 18-08
authored; a workspace wheel handler (see the record artifact — a named cut, and 18-09's file besides);
brightness on the hardware media keys, which already route through `swayosd-client` in `keybinds.lua`
and are Phase 20's to migrate; and DDC/external-monitor brightness, which `keybinds.lua`'s own D-25
comment puts out of scope for this whole project.

Also not this plan's: any edit to `Bar.qml` or `shell.qml`. The audio handle and the entry list are
sufficient, and needing either file would mean the 18-05 seam did not hold.
