<objective>
Build the section-popout frame and the complete hover contract behind QBAR-09, and prove both
end-to-end on the audio popout only.

This plan knowingly adds a **second frame** to a codebase whose existing frame's own header
forbids exactly that. `PanelDialog.qml` opens with the words "Every panel this phase and Phase 16
add is constructed FROM this type, never a bespoke `PanelWindow`". D-18-15 overrides that rule on
purpose, and records the cost on purpose: a second frame must be registered in GATE-03's
structural checks, covered by GATE-04's hex lint, and kept in visual and motion step with
`PanelDialog` **by review rather than by construction**. That is an accepted cost, not an
oversight, and the new file's header must say so in its own first paragraph so the next person to
open it meets the reasoning before the code.

`PanelDialog` is the **contrast case, not the template**. It is a fixed 850×620 window, anchored
top only and therefore compositor-centred, claiming no exclusive zone, on the overlay layer.
Reusing it would mean the bar's audio pill opens the identical centred dialog `Super+A` already
opens — QBAR-09 would deliver nothing. The popout is a **glance surface**: bounded 300–360px,
anchored off the entry that opened it, showing a compact summary plus primary quick actions, with
a wayfinding link at its foot to the full surface. The dashboard drawer keeps its full four-tab
role and the panel family keeps its unbounded lists (D-18-17); no shipped v3.0 surface is thinned
by this plan. Popouts reserve nothing.

Three things this plan delivers, in order:

1. **The tracer.** One path, every layer: a token in `Design.qml`, a type in the manifest, the
   frame, the controller, the trigger, the audio body, and the audio entry in 18-08's capsule
   wired to open it. Click the audio entry, the popout opens anchored under it, click outside and
   it goes away. Proven on the live host against `hyprctl layers -j`, not read in the source.
2. **The hover contract in full.** Preview suppressed until the reveal has settled and the pointer
   has moved once (D-18-19), `Design.popoutDwellMs` 400 dwell (D-18-20), `Design.popoutDismissGraceMs`
   200 grace over the combined entry-plus-popout region (D-18-21), click-to-pin that ignores hover
   thereafter (D-18-22), one open at a time through one guarded summon path.
3. **The glance-surface state grammar and the way out.** The four-state body vocabulary reused from
   `PanelDialog`, audio's pending state driven by a real backend signal, and the "Open in
   dashboard" link wired all the way through to the audio `PanelDialog`.

Output: four new QML types under `modules/bar/`, six appended `Design.qml` tokens, and a hover
contract 18-14 can hang five more bodies on without touching any of it.

**Explicitly NOT in this plan** (named so the executor does not helpfully build ahead):

- **No second popout body.** Wifi, bluetooth, clock, resources and media are 18-14's, and 18-14
  gets an unchanged frame. Building a sixth body here would make the tracer a batch.
- **No tray tooltip**, despite 18-10 naming this plan as the owner of the hover contract a tooltip
  would need. UI-SPEC E5 long-text states tray icons carry no text; this plan builds the contract
  and records that the tooltip itself remains unbuilt with no requirement asking for it.
- **No fullscreen refusal guard on the popout.** `openPanel()` owns that guard in exactly one
  place, and duplicating it into a second summon path is precisely the anti-pattern this repo has
  already corrected once. From 18-15 onward the bar is in `hidden-hard` under fullscreen, so no
  trigger is reachable to hover. Named as a carry-forward to 18-15, not a silent gap.
- **No change to any backend's gate.** The popout reads `AudioBackend` through 18-05's existing
  `BarCapsule` handle; 18-08 already widened `audioTruthNeeded` and paid that cost by name.
- **No hot zone, no reveal animation, no visibility owner.** `PopoutController.barSettled` is
  declared as the input 18-16 drives and defaults to true; the half of D-18-19 that is provable
  today — that a preview needs a pointer move on the settled bar — is provable today and is proven.
- **No new Hyprland layer rule.** The `^quickshell-.*` family blur / `ignore_alpha` pair already
  covers the new namespace. If a per-surface rule is ever added it must be declared AFTER that
  family regex, or it silently loses.
