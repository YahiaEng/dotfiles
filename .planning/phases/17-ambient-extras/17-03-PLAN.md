<objective>
Make the live wallpaper reachable, controllable and quiet: pick one from the picker, see it, cancel back out of it, and have it stop whenever nothing should be moving.

17-01 proved the player and 17-02 proved the frame pipeline around it. This plan is the surface — the picker that lists and previews live wallpapers, the one wiring path that starts them at login, on a theme switch and on a manual pick, the three states that suppress them, the coexistence assertion that proves the layer surface sits where it must, and the blocking human render gate that closes AMB-01 for standing constraint #1.

Purpose: this is the plan where AMB-01 becomes usable rather than demonstrable. It also closes two live handoffs 17-02 states explicitly — the picker cannot record a live choice until its writer-side guard is widened, and 17-02's repointing of `current.jpg` into the state directory breaks the picker's active-entry detection for every wallpaper, live or still. The second is a real regression introduced upstream in this same phase and it is fixed here with a criterion that would catch it.

Output: a merged picker list with marked live entries and cached frame previews; a debounced hover that cannot orphan a player; an Esc that restores exactly what was there; one `theme_engine_wallpaper_sync_owner` function serving all three entry paths; gaming/idle/reduced-motion suppression; a `quickshell-doctor` mpvpaper layer check with fixtures; and a blocking human render-and-look gate.

**Precedence note (RESEARCH.md corrects CONTEXT.md).** D-19's original framing carried a CPU objection to hover-starting the player. CONTEXT.md's own grounded correction retracts it: the measurement was never taken. The real objection is lifecycle churn and orphan risk — each fzf `focus:execute-silent` event becomes a process spawn plus a Wayland layer surface plus decoder init, unserialised, where today it is only IPC to an already-running daemon. Build the debounce for churn and orphans; do not carry the unmeasured CPU claim into a comment, a commit message or the SUMMARY.

**Plan-time finding that changes D-28's risk (record in the SUMMARY).** CONTEXT.md's `<deferred>` section flags `gaming-mode-toggle.sh` as possibly silently dead since the 13.1 Lua cutover. Read at plan time: it is not. Lines 113-116 and 164-188 already use `hyprctl eval` with `hl.config({...})`, migrated by 13.1-10 and confirmed live at that time. Only the file's header comment at line 6 still claims every compositor change goes through `hyprctl keyword` — a stale comment, which is exactly what the deferred item was reading. D-28's hook does not touch the compositor at all: it mirrors `_gaming_waybar_toggle`, a plain script call proven in production since Phase 8. The deferred `/gsd-debug` remains valid as its own item; D-28 is not blocked on it.

**Scope boundary — what this plan deliberately does NOT do.** It does not touch `install.sh`, `stow.sh`, `contract.json`, `theme-doctor`, `generate.sh`, `env.lua` or anything in the AMB-02 track. It adds no `autostart.lua` entry (D-15/D-35 no-new-entries prohibition). It plans no MPRIS suppression — RESEARCH Deep-Dive #6 settled D-16 as a non-issue because mpv core ships zero MPRIS support, so there is nothing to suppress. It does not re-implement frame extraction: every extraction in this plan calls 17-02's functions.

## The T-05-07 trio, now complete

The last-wallpaper value crosses three boundaries in this phase and each one has its own guard, deliberately, with no shared trust assumption:

- **17-02** constrains what may be *recorded and resolved* — `theme_engine_wallpaper_is_live_ref` on a relative recorded state value, paired with `-f` at every call site.
- **This plan** constrains what may be *written* — the picker's writer-side guard at line 346, delegating to that same function so the writer cannot admit a shape the reader would reject, or vice versa.
- **17-01** constrains what is *handed to the player* — `_validate_selection` on a resolved absolute path, normalised with `realpath -m --no-symlinks`, independent of both of the above.

Two of the three delegate to one shape function precisely so the writer and reader cannot drift; the third is deliberately independent because it validates a different input shape at a different boundary. None may be weakened on the grounds that the others exist.

**Not in this plan's scope, recorded so it is not assumed covered:** the sudo-scoped `hyprpm` build from a remote git repository belongs to **17-04**; compositor-payload argv discipline for the D-27 fallback watcher belongs to **17-01**; the frame destination path and the seek-offset override validation belong to **17-02**.
