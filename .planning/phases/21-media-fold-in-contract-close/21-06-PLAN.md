<objective>
Expand the proven audio slice into the full visualiser on both media surfaces, reshape
the cover art, and deliver the design-contract amendment that legalises the ring's colour.

The tracer proved one bar on one surface. This plan turns that into 60 bars around a
12-lobe scalloped cover on the dashboard Media tab, ports both to the bar's media popout
(which today has neither a mask nor a ring), and amends the drawer's design contract so
the code and the spec agree about the accent role.

The technique is not new machinery: the Media tab already imports the shapes module and
already draws its ring as a shape path with an angle arc, already declares the curve
renderer, and already animates its stroke colour. The ring becomes a repeater over that
same machinery. The mask mechanism is likewise unchanged — only its source shape changes.

Purpose: deliver QMEDIA-02 in full, on both surfaces, without losing the silence-state
appearance that the design was accepted on.
Output: the live ring on both surfaces, the reshaped art, and the amended contract.
</objective>
