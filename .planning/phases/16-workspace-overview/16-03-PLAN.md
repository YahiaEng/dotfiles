<objective>
Expand the tracer's single tile into D-16-01's fixed eleven-slot grid without
rewriting it: ten numbered tiles in the 5x2 arrangement that mirrors the number
row, plus D-16-05's visually separated scratchpad tile, each drawing its
workspace's windows at real scaled geometry, each carrying its workspace number,
empty state and orientation cues, and the whole block entering on a row-level
cascade.

The 5x2 arrangement is load-bearing, not cosmetic: `1 2 3 4 5` over `6 7 8 9 0`
mirrors the number row exactly, so D-16-01's spatial constancy teaches itself and
D-16-16's `Shift+1..0` window moves have somewhere to point. Fixed slots also mean
the drop target never moves between summons, which is what makes criterion 3's
aim-and-drop viable at all.

This plan also extracts the tracer's inline capture delegate into a first-class
`WindowThumbnail` type. That is the assumption-delta promotion made concrete:
one general representation of "a window drawn small", with the capture mode as a
variant, so D-16-07's fallback ladder and D-16-12's drag ghost are later property
changes rather than second renderers.

Purpose: OVER-01's "a full-screen grid of workspaces showing live thumbnails of
every open window" becomes literally true — including the scratchpad, without
which criterion 1 is silently false for any window sent there by Super+Shift+S.
Output: three QML files carrying the whole grid, its recorded fit arithmetic, and
a human-approved look.
</objective>
