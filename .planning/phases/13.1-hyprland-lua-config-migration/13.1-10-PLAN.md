restart, because the legacy tree stayed on disk and its generated inputs kept being produced. That

installed Hyprland moves to the release that removes hyprlang entirely — reverting stops being
possible at all on this machine. This is the phase's only genuine one-way door.

The evidence to weigh is in `13.1-EQUIVALENCE-REPORT.md`: the gate result and every reasoned
divergence, the soak evidence from Task 1, the per-property compensating observations, and the
recorded fact that permission grants could not be covered by the automated gate and rest instead on
the textual review in `13.1-PERMISSION-REVIEW.md`.

There is no deadline pressure on this specific step. The upstream clock forces the *migration*, not
the *deletion* — the Lua config is already live and already carrying the desktop.
